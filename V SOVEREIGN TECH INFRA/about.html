Corporate Overview

M/S. V-SOVEREIGN TECH-INFRA is a registered Partnership Firm established in June 2026 under the laws of Andhra Pradesh, India. Headquartered in Chandracherla, Sri Sathya Sai District, the firm was founded with a singular focus: to modernize regional infrastructure through dependable civil works, streamlined manpower logistics, and commercial transport operations.

As a partner-managed entity, we operate with a highly efficient capital structure and transparent governance. We bring together localized insight, modern machinery, and rigorous compliance to serve public and private developers alike.
